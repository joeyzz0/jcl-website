src/v1.5.0.md

update 1.5.0

frontiers & fashion

The biggest update the League has had. Here’s what’s waiting for you. Every change is listed in the patch notes.

port_azure.mapnew Port Azure

Spawn now sits on a brand new island. Walk the streets, parks, piers and beaches, visit the Pokémon Lab, Contest Hall and Oceanic Museum, shop two market squares, and find your name in the Hall of Champions.

battle_frontier.datnew Battle Frontier

A whole island of 34 battle rooms, reached on the S.S. Seabreeze once you earn your Ticket to the Frontier. Singles and Doubles from Rookie to Master, seven Frontier Brains, and BP to spend at the BP Exchange.

style.zipnew Style and Wardrobe

Trails, auras and outfits from the Style Crate, a Wardrobe to change your look, and 142 Style Ticket looks for your Pokémon. Over 200 cosmetics are free, and every one can be earned in game.

quest_book.jsonnew JCL Quest Book

Our own quest book, opened with L or /quests. Your progress came with you, plus Claim All, a Requires list on every quest, and new Jobs and Stats tabs.

badge_box.datnew Badges and Badge Box

A badge for every gym challenge, each with its own art. The new Badge Box keeps a record of your battles. Wear it, open it with Shift+B, and dye it any colour.

challenge.shnew /challenge

Battle any player in Singles, Doubles or Triples at any level, with an optional wager and rules like Smogon Singles and VGC. Your real Pokémon never gain or lose anything.

pokerus.binnew Pokérus

Rare, contagious, and it doubles EV gains. Look for PKRS on the summary screen, or check /pokerus to see if your team caught it.

calendar.cfgnew League calendar

Four seasons, twelve months, holidays and full moons. Weather follows the season, and trees and grass turn from spring blossom to autumn colours to winter snow. See /calendar.

crates.cfgnew Crates reworked

New rewards in every crate, from shiny Pokémon and perfect Dittos to Paradox Pokémon, Legendaries and a shiny Mythical in the Master Crate. Click any Random reward to see what it can be.

src/install.md

install guide

Pick the launcher you use. Each guide takes you from nothing to standing in Port Azure.

  1. Get the app

    Download the CurseForge app from curseforge.com/download/app. The standalone version works fine.

  2. Install the pack

    Open the pack’s CurseForge page and press Install, then let your browser open the app. You can also search Joey’s Cobblemon League under Browse Modpacks in the app.

  3. Press All Files required

    CurseForge asks whether to install CurseForge Files Only or All Files. Press All Files. The pack includes files that aren’t hosted on CurseForge, and without them it won’t work.

  4. Give it more memory

    Go to Settings › Minecraft › Java Settings and set Allocated Memory to 8192 MB (6144 MB at the very least).

  5. Launch and join

    Press Play. In Multiplayer, the League is pinned to the top of your server list. If it isn’t there, choose Add Server and enter joeyscobblemonleague.com.

Updating from an older version? Open My Modpacks, press Update on Joey’s Cobblemon League, and press All Files again if it asks.

coming soonThe Modrinth version isn’t out yet. Use CurseForge for now, and watch the Discord for the release.

  1. Get the app

    Download the Modrinth App from modrinth.com/app and sign in with your Microsoft account.

  2. Install the pack

    Search Joey’s Cobblemon League in the app’s modpack browser and press Install. If you downloaded the .mrpack file, use the + button and import it from file instead.

  3. Press Install anyway required

    The Modrinth App warns that the pack contains files that aren’t hosted on Modrinth. Press Install anyway. If you cancel, the pack is missing files and won’t work.

  4. Give it more memory

    Open the instance’s settings, go to Java and memory, and set memory to 8192 MB.

  5. Launch and join

    Press Play. In Multiplayer, the League is pinned to the top of your server list. If it isn’t there, choose Add Server and enter joeyscobblemonleague.com.

  1. Get Prism

    Download Prism Launcher from prismlauncher.org and add your Microsoft account under Accounts.

  2. Add the pack

    Choose Add Instance › CurseForge, search Joey’s Cobblemon League and press OK. If Prism lists mods it can’t download for you, open each link, save the file to the folder Prism shows, then continue.

  3. Give it more memory

    Right-click the instance, choose Edit › Settings › Java, tick Memory and set the maximum to 8192 MiB.

  4. Launch and join

    Launch the instance. In Multiplayer, the League is pinned to the top of your server list. If it isn’t there, choose Add Server and enter joeyscobblemonleague.com.

Stuck on a step? Post a screenshot in the Discord and someone will walk you through it.

src/faq.yml

faq

Quick answers. For anything else, ask in the Discord.

q:Is the server free?
a:

Yes. Install the modpack, log in with your Minecraft: Java Edition account and join.

q:Is it pay-to-win?
a:

No, and it never will be. Over 200 cosmetics are free, and every one can be earned in game. Nothing that gives you an edge is for sale.

q:When is the server open?
a:

24/7. It restarts every 12 hours, with warnings in chat and on Discord first.

q:CurseForge asked which files to install. Which one?
a:

All Files, every time. CurseForge Files Only skips files the pack needs, and it won’t work. When the Modrinth version is out, its warning has an Install anyway button: press that.

q:Which version do I need?
a:

Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge, through the modpack. Install it from CurseForge rather than building your own mod list, so everything matches the server.

q:How do I update to 1.5.0?
a:

In the CurseForge app, open My Modpacks and press Update on Joey’s Cobblemon League. Press All Files again if it asks. From now on, pack updates won’t overwrite your settings and keybinds.

q:How much memory should I give the game?
a:

8 GB is the sweet spot and 6 GB is the minimum. Going far past 8 GB rarely helps.

q:How do I get to the Battle Frontier?
a:

Ride the S.S. Seabreeze from Port Azure. You’ll need a Ticket to the Frontier first: open your quest book with L, go to the Jobs tab, and it tells you who to talk to.

q:Some of my items disappeared after 1.5.0.
a:

Legendary Monuments items, Simply Swords’ runic and unique weapons, and every item from a removed mod were deleted from all inventories and containers. The removed mods are listed in the patch notes.

q:Where did hostile mobs go?
a:

They no longer spawn, apart from Endermen for XP. Winning battles now gives more XP to make up for it.

q:My keybinds changed.
a:

Keybinds were remapped in 1.5.0 so every key does one thing. Type /keys reset to get them back.

q:Why can’t my Pokémon level up past a certain point?
a:

There’s a level cap tied to your trainer progress. Beat more trainers in the trainer series to raise it. The starter chapter of the quest book explains how it works.

q:How do gyms and badges work?
a:

Every trainer series has its own gym challenge. Beat a gym leader to earn its badge, and keep them all in your Badge Box (Shift+B). Player-run gyms are coming soon.

q:Where did Waystones go?
a:

Homes replaced them. Use /sethome and /home. Everyone gets 3 for free and can buy more with CobbleDollars, up to 30. Want your build on the Community Warps menu? Ask a staff member.

q:Can I add my own mods or shaders?
a:

The pack is built to match the server. If you want to add a client-side extra like shaders, ask in the Discord first.

q:I found a bug, or my game crashes.
a:

Post it in the Discord with your crash report or latest.log. If it crashes on launch, check that you pressed All Files when installing and that the game has 8 GB of memory.
